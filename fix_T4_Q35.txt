You use Microsoft Defender for Cloud for the centralized policy management of three Azure subscriptions.
You use several policy denitions to manage the security of the subscriptions.
You need to deploy the policy denitions as a group to all three subscriptions.
Solution: You create a resource graph and an assignment that is scoped to a management group.
Does this meet the goal?
A. Yes
B. No
Correct Answer:
B
Management groups in Microsoft Azure solve the problem of needing to impose governance policy on more than one Azure subscription
simultaneously.
However, you need to use an initiative, not a resource graph to bundle the policy denitions into a group that can be applied to the
management group.
